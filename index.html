    CAPAS is an open layer of art over the streets. Pieces are pinned to real coordinates and woken up by real anchors: a GPS radius, a sticker, a tag, a wall.

    1. No landlords. Every piece is plain JSON under a free license. Fork the wall.
    2. You have to be there. Street pieces only open in place. Presence is the price of entry.
    3. Many layers, one city. STREET, DREAM, MEMORY, SIGNAL. Toggle realities.
    4. Nobody deletes the past. Pieces can be painted over, never erased. History stays in the repo.
    5. Brands pay rent, not rules. Institutions can sponsor layers and routes. They don't get to own the protocol.
