# Ideas parking lot

Raw ideas, not decisions. We decant them into the roadmap later.

## Who sees a piece (visibility)

| Visibility | Who sees it | Who can post |
|---|---|---|
| Mine | only you | you |
| Crew | you and your friends | any crew member |
| Project | public | invited artists only (curated drops) |
| Institution | public, marked as theirs | the institution's curators |
| Public | everyone | anyone (with moderation) |

Layers (STREET, DREAM, MEMORY, SIGNAL) say *what kind of reality* a piece is; visibility says *whose* it is.

## Crews

- Make a community with your friends and see your own pieces on the map.
- A crew's wall: only members can paint there.

## Collecting ("pokedex")

- You *catch* pieces by physically going there (inside the radius, or scanning the QR/NFC anchor).
- Your collection shows everything you've caught: where, when, which layer.
- Some pieces are exclusive: limited numbers, only during an event, only for a crew, only the first N people who show up.
- Owning a piece could unlock things: a hidden layer, a second version, the artist's next drop, an event.

Open questions:
- What does "own" mean here? A badge in your collection, a limited edition, a transferable item, or a print/physical object?
- Does collecting reward the artist (money, visibility, data on who came)?
- How to stop GPS spoofing from faking a catch (QR/NFC anchors help; a moving code on a screen helps more).
- Scarcity vs. the open-source, "no landlords" principle: exclusivity of *experience* (you were there) fits better than exclusivity of *property*.
